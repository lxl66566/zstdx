## Item notes

### 1. MT decode scaling

Per-job content-adaptive D is the remaining structural idea. Pieces fully unchain only at D at or above the window, where crossing reads drop to zero. Between that point and the piece size, floor(D/J) pieces chain back through overlap. Below one piece the job is serial-chain. Ratio demands a D under the shape's dominant match period, and text-9 has a period of 800 KiB against a 4 MiB window, so no D satisfies it. Unpledged streams stay excluded because the growing grid's tail split depends on the encoder's worker count.

The landed walls cover the json mt8 verdict and execute stages, and they are memory-bound floors. No wall lever remains short of overlapping execute with the verdict, which is a falsified class. A beyond-noise regression on the piece-parallel path was traced to one decoder commit that fused bit reads, and that commit was reverted. The fused body's six-widths-six-bases live set fits only the flat executor's loop, and the staging pass spilled it. The per-callsite `const FUSED` monomorphization later restored the ST stream cells. Details are in [mt-stream](perf/mt-stream.md) and [negative](negative/mt-stream.md).

### 3. Fastest json scan

json reads x1.45 ST and x1.19 mt8, at 590 against 852 MiB/s. The steady scan loop is branch and latency bound at IPC near 3. The mispredict budget is fully attributed in [matchers](perf/matchers.md): 2.22M misses per encode take about 12% of cycles, `insert_covered`'s fill carried the only removable lump at 26% to 28% and its harvest is falsified through `DenseFlat`, each extend costs 2% or less, the OR-fold fires in 56% of the 2.35M pair iterations, and nothing else exceeds 12%.

The per-event instruction decomposition put matcher and fill at 2.25x C while the entropy side wins at 0.82x, and the resolve block is the largest single lump at 13%. The structural levers are exhausted. The u32 idx-space resolve and the register-removal class in both cursor forms are falsified. The table geometry was swept and closed: 2^13 and 2^14 buy json cycles but cost text +6.0% and +2.2% and dll100 +6.9% and +4.4%, and 2^16 inverts. 2^15 is the ratio-chosen optimum, and the 1.5x per-event instruction volume is its closed price. A new lever must ship as a dense-mode instantiation that keeps the plain body byte for byte, because miss-run stepping and batched probing are falsified as unconditional changes.

A former defect past 2^32 plus max_window of a stream, where the u64 resolve rejected all hash candidates, is closed. The fast and dfast scans run in a rebased coordinate epoch through `fast_shift`, which costs 2% or less Ir on dense shapes and leaves the u64 resolve untouched. The same per-event codegen deficit caps the armed dll100 fast rows, where event volume, memory and armed machinery are at parity or better and about 98 against 71 Ir per probed position is the whole gap. The json.fastest.mt8 gap is this per-core deficit scaled through workers.

### 4. Small and mid payloads

The level-9 flip was the small-source btlazy swap, and it is now a head verdict over 8 KiB of `sampled_distinct` with a bar of 48. json and skewed keep the stock row, and text, dll and random keep the swap. The gated cells read json l9 at x1.13, 0.95, 0.64 and 1.09 for 1K, 4K, 64K and 1M. text l9 keeps the swap bytes at x1.73, 1.48, 2.78 and 1.08, with ratio 0.9% better than zstd-9 at 64K. The level-9 band is closed as a deliberate per-class trade.

Level 1 still slides at 1K to 4K and on text at 64K, at x1.21 to x1.66. The cause is the tiny dense bars above 8 KiB for fast and dfast, whatever the alphabet, plus the fast row's small-source dense bars. The slide is an accepted ratio trade. Level 3 holds near parity at x0.98 to 1.29. The exact sequence-selection arm's stock-row exemption covers json l9 and l10 at 1K with 4% to 6% better size at byte-identical corpus sizes. The 1M cells belong to the bulk tier and to item 3. See [matchers](perf/matchers.md).

### 17. dll100 streaming-MT encode

The first measurement fed T5b through `t5_payloads`. On the 100 MB binary payload, stream-mt8 against libzstd-mt8 leads only at fast and opt, at x0.81 and x0.96, and trails fastest x2.36, balanced x1.15 and best x3.12. It runs at 86% to 93% of zstdx's own bulk-mt8 ceilings: fastest 897 MiB/s of 1048, best 27 of 29, ultra 18 at parity x0.98, and balanced 161. The best tier's original collapse was the long-compare regime. `jobdecomp` put 94.9% of job time in `DubtFinder::find_best`, at about 1.15x the ST search count but 28x the compared bytes, 4084 against 144 B per `count_from` call. dll's multi-KiB twin regions were re-compared full-length against the jobs' shallow, budgeted-sort trees, while the deep ST tree skips them through its high prefix-start offsets.

`count_from_long` is a SIMD compare with AVX-512BW and AVX2 kernels that compare 64 or 32 B per step. It is selected by a `const LONG` monomorphization of `DubtFinder` and the btlazy parse, armed on the job-arming context alone. It is a pure function of the window bytes, so it is byte-exact by construction. The 120-cell sweep is byte-identical, the forced scalar, AVX2 and AVX-512 builds are identical, and the ST rows run the stock scalar loop with no per-call dispatch cost. Gungraun best reads -1.2%, +0.2% and -1.2% Ir on json, text and skewed, which is layout noise. The mechanism and gates are in [mt-stream](perf/mt-stream.md) and [matchers](perf/matchers.md).

The remaining gaps were re-attributed to the accumulate buffer's mid-stream growth barrier. `make_space` grows only at full quiescence, so the schedule-scale decided reserve of 96 MiB on the engaged capture and 36 MiB on the Growing grid serialized buffer-sized waves. On fastest, the pump parked for 100 of 176 ms and the last 16 MiB job ran alone. The deep rows drained whole Growing epochs behind 36 and 72 MiB barriers. `reserve_decided` now covers the pledged extent and the full 256 MiB cap, and `BUF_POOL_KEEP_MAX` rose to the cap to remove the re-fault bimodality. The change is pure scheduling and every output byte is identical: the st and mt4 dump, the 120-cell sweep and the dll100 stream cells at w4, w8 and w16 are base-equal.

The remaining fastest 14% is the four concurrent 16 MiB jobs' whole-window LDM cold fills plus dll's content spread. Smaller final-band jobs are the measured band-count falsification, which lands up to +24% size at four or five band jobs. Ultra's 18% is two straggler parses with summed over eight at the balance floor. Zstd's 2051 MiB/s headline buys 48 MB of output against our 24 MB, so the comparison is not iso-ratio. Data is in the encode streaming table in [matrix](bench/matrix.md).

### 18. compat knobs without an `EncoderOptions` field

`include_dictid(bool)` landed as `EncoderOptions::include_dictid`, which suppresses the header field while the dictionary stays in effect. The options-based decode entries, `DecoderOptions::dictionary`, `DecoderOptions::parsed_dictionary` and the compat constructors, register the dictionary under the null id too, so a suppressed frame decodes when the dictionary is handed over. Libzstd applies a loaded dictionary unconditionally. The bulk MT dictionary path cannot express the suppression and demotes to the single-threaded dictionary entry, which is the same kind of MT engagement demotion as the small-input one. The FFI `ZSTD_c_dictIDFlag` is wired.

The remaining knobs are in place. `long_distance_matching(bool)` maps onto the `EncoderOptions::long_distance_matching(LdmMode)` tri-state, because the upstream bool expresses enable and auto only. `Enable` raises the window to 27 when none is forced, and `Disable` stands the arming down. The setting is wired through bulk and stream ST, the compat setters and FFI `ZSTD_c_enableLongDistanceMatching`. Both MT dispatches carry the tri-state into every job: `Disable` stands the capture lattice down, including dict frames and the stream far-dead screens, and `Enable` forces every job with the fast-row capture off, while `st_fallback` and the ST stream core keep the mode. `with_ref_prefix` on both stream sides is the raw-dictionary path under libzstd's prefix name. `ZSTD_c_refPrefix` loads raw content, which is exactly the engine's raw-dict load: no entropy, null id and format-default repcodes. Decode-side prefix registration rides the null-id dictionary work. The fidelity notes in the [user guide](../user.md) compat deviations still hold: an explicit enable below the 64 MiB full window leaves raw-block prefixes unindexed through the split pass's stock exemption, and the tagged rows at levels 5-8 and 10-12 have no LDM feed in their parse.

`set_target_cblock_size` stays unimplemented by verdict. The splitting machinery loses ratio on every measured shape and serves only fixed-size transport chunks, which this engine does not model. See [negative](negative/entropy.md).

Dictionary re-parse residues are closed. The compat `bulk::Compressor` raw attachment resolves its parse lazily at the first compression and caches the outcome until any re-set. Error timing stays: an invalid dictionary errors at the first compress call and at every later one, replayed from the cached raw bytes. `FrameCompressor::set_parsed_dictionary` shares one parse across frames, while the raw `set_dictionary` keeps its per-frame parse and panic contract. The other byte-dictionary resolvers, `bulk::compress_with`, the stream cores and the compat stream encoders' single materialization, are audited to one parse per use.

Before the cache, the re-parse cost 12.4, 14.7 and 13.6 µs per call at levels 1, 3 and 9 on 4 KiB payloads with a 110 KiB formatted dictionary. Of that, 9.0 µs was the parse and the rest was the per-call `options.dictionary` byte copy, and the cost was invisible at 19. The per-call dictionary cost is now a cache hit. Pooled ST dictionary states restore a captured post-seed image of tables, filled trees and entropy, keyed on the dictionary identity, the level, the LDM mode and the shape's seeding projection. The per-call dictionary tax is 5.8 µs at level 1 and 16.5 µs at level 3, and the seeding itself takes 3.5 to 16 µs at every level. The residuals are the dictionary-row parse at 9 and the lazy tree fill at 13 and 19. See [encoding](perf/encoding.md).

The cache key's exact-shape slot is replaced by the seeding's own decision projection, `dict_seed_key`, which covers the resolved params, `dict_row` and `LitStreams`. Payload sizes inside one projection class share the image, so a repeated-dictionary workload with varying record sizes stays on the hit path. A loop over varying sizes in [4K, 5K) reads 20, 32, 218, 904 and 959 µs at levels 1, 3, 9, 13 and 19, with every output byte identical to the uncached path.

### 19. Dictionary-frame parse residuals

The seed-image cache eagerly fills the tree families over the dictionary content. Opt rows use `update_tree` with the ultra rows' frame-start statistics parse replayed first, and the replay shapes `opt_state` so the frame skips its own `initStats`. Btlazy rows use the DUBT `fill_range` with the deferred heads clear run eagerly. The tables and the opt coordinate origin are canonicalized to zero beforehand, so the image is a pure function of the cache key and captures the filled tree. A restore is one sequential table copy instead of the first block's random-access fill. The per-call dictionary tax on 512 calls of 4 KiB over the 52 KiB fixture dictionary is 0.58 ms at level 13, 0.57 ms at 15 and -0.15 ms at 19, where the dictionary frame outruns the plain one. The numbers and the fill and copy cost model are in [encoding](perf/encoding.md).

The fill stays byte-neutral by construction. Three exceptions are deliberate. Opt rows with dictionary content past the stock search domain keep the lazy fill, because the frame's LDM alphabet gate parks LDM at the first block and moves the fill floor. Btlazy rows on small-source spans keep it, because the tree hash width is screened over the dictionary plus the block. An ultra dictionary frame whose first block is 8 bytes or less parses differently, because the lazy flow skips its seed parse there while the eager replay has already shaped the prices. That frame still has a legal parse.

Level 9 keeps a tax of about 54 µs per call from the dictionary-row x8 chain walk through the dictionary's dense twin field. The cost is candidate density, against libzstd's 40 µs, and it is likely not tractable. The 13 and 15 residue in the 4 KiB loop is now parse-side: the swapped small-dictionary row's dense parse plus the 4096-position tail fill. MT job zero keeps the plain per-call seeding by verdict, because dict-MT payloads are multi-job inputs where the fill is noise. A 32 MiB payload at 19 amortizes the 6 ms fill to under 0.3%, while the cross-thread `DICT_KIT` would carry four stock-row images, about 320 MiB at 19, for that gain.

The projection key lands the varying-size half. Size changes inside a projection class no longer re-seed, and the cache-carving and threshold ladder is gate-pinned by `dict_seed_key_mirror_sweep`, so a new length read in the seeding fails the sweep instead of corrupting restores.

### 6. Greedy -4 dictionary row

On the 3-seed trainer re-baseline, every dictionary level is ahead except -4, at 13 to 96 B per seed, and -3 with a CLI-formatted dictionary. The -4 residue is fragmentation with no isolated mechanism. The -3 CLI-formatted cell is the blind-repeat favorable-deviation case, and porting it was falsified. The remaining shape is the exact-once insert discipline: fill the rep-advance holes and never re-insert a head. The frontier-skip form was landed and reverted in the same round because it regressed every dictionary row. See [negative](negative/matchers.md).

Everything else in this area is closed or falsified. The small-payload row swaps at 16 KiB and below, chain 4-8 and opt 11-22 with tuned-past-the-table knobs, and libzstd's lazy-plus three-way sequence selection for the small-frame entropy tail are in place. The trainer's k-grid and finalize-stats defects are fixed, and content is ahead of `zstd --train` at every seed and level in raw form and in formatted totals, so future dictionary-row measurements re-baseline from that trainer. The -11/-12 btopt-row, -6 to -8 and -1 to -2 residues do not reproduce under same-dict pairing. Own-dictionary content varies by about ±440 B across train splits, and the per-file events are same-position alternate picks rather than absent candidates, so they are falsified and must not be re-scanned. See [encoding](perf/encoding.md), [negative](negative/matchers.md), [negative](negative/entropy.md) and [negative](negative/dict.md).

### 22. FFI parity coverage

The FFI unprobed families are estimate, static, advanced, ref*, block-level and the push API. That is about 87 symbols deliberately left out. The streaming block-granular staging also deviates: `destFull` arrives at call 16 against the reference's 17. Matching it needs an internal decode-ahead redesign. See [ffiparity](review/ffiparity.md).

The rest of the follow-ups are closed. The CLI `-l` level-3 residue is the dfast row's recorded near-tie band, confirmed by a size and shape sweep around parity, and it is not a defect. Pass-through decode landed with `--pass-through` and `--no-pass-through` and the `-f` plus stdout default, over a fileio-style frame loop. That loop exposed and fixed `single_frame` consuming the next frame's header past the boundary. `--train` small-corpus warnings landed as DiB's level-2 pair plus `COVER_warnOnSmallCorpus`'s level-1 line over `TrainOutcome.dmers`. See [cliparity](review/cliparity.md).

### 23. Fuzz campaign for window-adoption edges

`smoke.sh` keeps the libFuzzer default `max_len` of 4096. The long-payload campaign uses `-max_len=300000 -len_control=20`. Its first pass ran 2026-10-24 as two 25-minute runs with eight ASAN workers over encode and encode_stream, and found no crashes. The corpora grew 2x to 6x and were re-merged. The remaining limit is that evolved inputs carry no beyond-window redundancy. Pre-campaign seeds are all 4096 B or smaller, and coverage-guided mutation rarely builds multi-KiB-period structure, so the window-adoption edges that a dict-seeded corpus would reach stay cold. That variant remains the lever. A forced-window interop bug sat inside this surface and needed hand-built probes to see.

The variant ran on 2026-10-24 with committed seed constructors and walker replays. The constructors cover beyond-window farfrags and twinhalves, dictionary content echoed past the window, dictionary and frame pairs through the options attach, and the forced-window ladder from w10 to w16. Two runs of 420 to 600 seconds with eight ASAN workers covered encode, encode_stream, decode and decode_dict at the same flags and found no library bugs. The corpora were coverage-merged, with decode going from 15672 to 993 seeds, decode_dict from 10699 to 843, encode from 7389 to 5290, encode_stream from 4745 to 3473, and dict_pooled to 2825. The single artifact was a harness-oracle defect rather than a decoder bug, adjudicated in [workflow pitfalls](pitfalls/workflow.md). The recipe is in the [fuzz Readme](../../../crates/zstdx-fuzz/Readme.md).

### 24. Hardening follow-ups

The balanced json +0.44% Ir residue is placement-only. A 4x order-swapped interleaved wall A/B on `prof enc 9` json is flat to positive, with a base median of 155.5 against 159 MiB/s and every pair favoring the newer build. The memory-bound chain tier never pays the extra instructions, so no placement recovery was spent.

The remaining compile-time menu holds A/B-gated refactors of hot code. Cross-domain index newtypes would keep `WinPos` and `TableIdx` from ever mixing. `NonZero` distance at the resolve sites would make distance 0 unrepresentable. Enum rep-bases would replace off-base integers. The `Option<Candidate>` arm landed on 2026-10-24 over the btlazy selection, recorded in [hardening](review/hardening.md).

The owned window's committed-length to staged-end carry zone is caller-live by the pre-split convention. Walling it needs a caller-contract change, which is not worth doing until a bug lands there. `parse_chain`'s `next - 512` wrap is flagged byte-load-bearing with a do-not-fix note in the tree.

### 27. compat bulk parameter methods

The compat bulk `Compressor` and `Decompressor` lack the parameter methods the zstd 0.13 crate gives them: `include_checksum`, `include_contentsize`, `window_log`, `multithread` on the encoder and `window_log_max` on the decoder. The compat stream types already carry the set, and `EncoderOptions` and `DecoderOptions` hold every knob, so the work is bulk shapes routing into the existing setters; the `window_log_max` range check currently lives in `compat/stream.rs` and moves to a shared module when the bulk setter lands.

## Identified floors

- Streaming decode reads json x1.13 to x1.33, skewed x1.08 to x1.28, text x1.04 to x1.20 and dll x1.28 to x1.34. The fused bit-read is in place as a per-callsite `const FUSED` monomorphization: the flat executor is fused, and the staging split stays machine-code-identical. The real-offset constant rotation is also in place, with json stream at -1.1% to -1.6%, skewed.zst3/9 stream at -2.4% and -3.0%, dll100 at +1% to +1.4% and text flat. The fused loop's cycle gap is pure dispatched-op volume under identical front-end service: op-cache occupancy is 99.5% on both sides, the loop buffer is unused, the front-end stall differential is at most 2.4 cycle-equivalent percentage points, and the IBS fetch latency in the loop is near zero. Every instruction-volume, structural and layout lever is falsified or measured out. See [decoding](perf/decoding.md) and [dec-gap](bench/dec-gap.md).

- json.fast reads x1.02 and is near closed. The residue is latency-bound concat and writer tails. The SIMD histogram ceiling is about 0.02% of wall, and the dfast scan's excess is register-budget-bound.
- json.best reads x1.37 and opt and ultra read x1.10 to x1.12. The floor is per-node codegen and event volume, measured to the factor: 1.20x the search count, which is the +2.5% and +12.8% json and dll density over zstd-13, times 1.39x Ir per call at parity L1d and branch misses and better IPC. The residue is distributed as 2-instruction to 3-instruction licks per tree event, and the events are the density mechanism. - skewed.best reads about x2.13 on a latency-bound walk at IPC 0.54 against C's 0.74. The descent-prefetch lever is falsified because speculation already saturates the walk's per-step memory-level parallelism. The head-load hoist is the only software-visible slack the walk had.
- The 100 MB binary ST encode has its armed far-class cells at speed parity or ahead, carrying double-digit size wins. Fastest reads x1.01 at 49.6% smaller output than zstd-1, and fast reads x0.95 at 31.7% smaller than zstd-3. - random fastest and fast read x0.91 and x0.85, and zeros fastest and fast read x0.31 and x0.21. These are the far-class screens' tax on compressible-flat data, and both cells still win.
- Small-payload ratio residues remain in accepted-tie classes. json l1 at 2K and 4K is 12 and 27 B larger from literal volume at sizes where the bars already match libzstd's rows. json 1K l9 is 11 B larger from the same class one family up. The text and json l19 tails are 1 to 3 B of stream ordering over equal-count parses. The "desc bytes at tiny sizes" attribution is falsified for these cells. See [encoding](perf/encoding.md).
- text.balanced bulk ST reads x1.10 to x1.23. The probe's shrink parse runs under the keep donation through `measure_shrink_concurrent`. The residue is the DUBT head parse itself, because a shallower head parses worse and hands the chain worse reps, and S4 is the whole value. The stream-mt tail is closed at 931 MiB/s, which is x1.06, and bulk-mt8 and mt16 are at zstd-mt parity at x1.03 and x1.02. Gap numbers are from the 2026-10-18 release snapshot ([snapshot](bench/snapshot.md)).

## Closed-item notes

### Item 5b

The width-4 dictionary-row semantics disarm above a 256 KiB span of payload plus dictionary, following libzstd's own rSize key and table boundary, while the x8 walk depth stays armed at every dictionary span. The depth converts with the stock width, and the width was the whole dilution. On 32 MiB json, dict-ST reads -2.4% at -3 and -16.3% at -9, where -9 is now 1.5% smaller than plain ST. text is flat except -9 at +1.3%, and the fixture grid is byte-identical. The span test left two holes that were closed: the small-payload row swap at 16 KiB and below re-armed the width above the ceiling, and the unknown-span arm ignored the dictionary's own size, where libzstd wraps its rSize key to dictSize plus 499. The MT capture and reach classes arm on dictionary frames wherever the schedule is a pure function of the source bytes that jobs above zero borrow. That covers the fast-row far screen and capture, the chain mid-size capture, the shared prefix fill and the stream far-dead screens, with the seed confined to job zero's owned window and job zero running the stock dictionary path on the armed grid. The reach probe stays off dictionary frames as a falsification, because SHRINK's W12 makes every dictionary-distance match illegal and destroys the dictionary. Far-class dictionary payloads convert like plain ones: on dll32, dict-mt4 against dict-ST is -63.8% for fastest, -27.9% for fast and +0.3% for balanced, with mt4 equal to mt8 and dict-mt4 walls at -10% to -42%. json and text dictionary sentinels and every plain output are byte-identical. The ST streaming core's unpledged dictionary fold, `shape.len.unwrap_or(0) + dict_len`, clamped an open-ended dictionary stream to a dictionary-sized window, and it now keeps `None` like the MT path, covered by `unpledged_dict_stream_keeps_full_window`.

### Item 8

Every decode dispatch site is BMI2-gated: the fused flat executor, the two-pass sequence decode and the MT segment decode. AVX2 entered silicon together with BMI2 at Haswell in 2013 and Zen in 2017, and the two have never been separated since, while AVX2 has no scalar variable-shift form, so the vector batching class is falsified. Every AVX2-capable CPU already runs the top decode tier, and an intermediate AVX2 tier has no selectable CPU, because the scalar fallback's population of pre-Haswell and pre-Zen machines has no AVX2 either. The BMI2 tier is forced through `ZSTDX_DEC_SIMD_TIER=scalar|bmi2`, where unset means detect.
