# Performance Optimization · Decoding

> Everything is landed and verifiable by commit; disproven directions are in [Disproven Directions](../negative.md); open residual work is in [TODO List](../todo.md).

## Output path evolution

1. **ring buffer**: hand-written ringbuffer ("discard from head" and "copy internally to tail" each need half the work; VecDeque/Vec can each do only one efficiently), with modulo-free wrap and hand-written extend_from_within for four layouts. Now used only by dictionary frames.
2. **flat direct write (slice)**: dictionary-free slice decoding writes Raw/RLE/Compressed blocks directly into the caller's target buffer, bypassing the ring and drain copies; block-level budget check + `TargetTooSmall` error variant. slice +13-35% across the board. `77b03b6`
3. **streaming outBuff model**: libzstd outBuff model, steady-state capacity window+2·block+64, two-generation virtual-mapping wrap (`FlatView{origin, prev_origin}` maintains a globally monotonic virtual address), zero sliding copies, flush leaves no window behind. Large-window corpora (8MB window) +85-125%, random/zeros overtake zstd-strm. `8426ba8`

## Fused sequence loop (current core)

- **decode-execute fusion** (libzstd model): sequences execute immediately after decoding, no round-trip through a sequence vector. Note that pure fusion was initially -1.8%; it only turned positive once faking RLE as a single-state table (fake entry self-transition, acc_log=0) eliminated 3 Option registers + 6 branches per sequence, while also deleting 4 variant loops. json.zst1 +10% (overtaking zstd crate slice), json.zst3 +7%. `971b43f`
- state-carrying decode_step + pointer-cursor executor + `HEADROOM` const specialization (when streaming buffers guarantee block size +16B slack, the per-sequence budget gate and wildcopy gate disappear entirely): streaming +4-7.4%. `c6e5fd9`
- the loop carries only three FSE states (not packed entries); `bits`/`src_len` leave the stream state (reload can always rebuild the bit window from memory); the executor cursor becomes raw pointers, folding away base.
- **register wall law**: with ≥15 live values in this loop, LLVM stack-spills the bit window (on the annotated asm, a `shlx` with a memory operand is the tell). All structural rework (splitting out batching, two-stage pipelines, register-resident repcodes) was disproven — the fused structure is locally optimal, see [Disproven Directions](../negative.md).
- **throughput-bound attribution (2026-09-14, from the ring-pipeline falsification — variants and per-shape losses in [negative](../negative.md))**: whole-decode IPC 3.45 (json.zst3) with a flat cycle profile (no instruction above ~3%) — the loop is **throughput-bound, not latency-bound**, so decode N+1 already overlaps exec N through OOO for free, and added bookkeeping (the three FSE states' stack round-trips, the ring store/load, the prefetch-cursor add chain) converts ~1:1 into wall. The loop's L1-miss clusters (wildcopy match source ~8%, its address math ~4.5%, bitstream reload ~4%, FSE entry loads ~3%) all hide behind that overlap — which is also why match-source prefetch buys nothing (−5% uniformly). Consequence: the remaining gap to libzstd is instruction quality, and ring/prefetch restructures are falsified.

- **SIMD copy scheduling falsified (2026-09-15)**: porting libzstd's `ZSTD_wildcopy` issue structure onto the existing kernels shrank the exec half by exactly the predicted 0.5-0.6M Ir (json) yet lost net Ir and wall on every shape — the loop is one register-allocation domain, and the saturated budget returns the copy-side saving as decode-half spills (+0.6-0.8M). The copy structure is at parity with libzstd's; the residue is decode-half instruction quality under allocation fragility (every source change to either half re-rolls the whole loop's allocation, ±600-800K Ir on json). Data in [negative.md](../negative.md).

- **fused bit-read (2026-09-19)**: the add-bits group and the transition group are consecutive in the bitstream and jointly bounded (sum ≤ 31 implies sum + sum_t ≤ 57 — exactly the coverage the post-reload window guarantees), so one window read serves both; the last sequence (no transitions) reads the add-bits alone, and the wide sum > 31 path is outlined cold. The win spill's store→load forwarding round trip sat on the serial bit path **twice** per sequence; fusing leaves one. Result: Ir rises (json +4.6%, text +4.4%, skewed +2.0% — a re-roll inside the documented ±600-800K band that spills the bases and sequence values) while IPC rises to match (json 3.462 → 3.565), so wall improves on every sequence-dense shape: json stream +1.7-2.0%, dll100 +2.0-2.8%, text.zst1 +1.9%, long-sequence shapes flat. Attribution refinement: the loop is throughput-bound in uops, but the win spill round trip is itself a serial dependency — trading spill uops (off-chain, cheap) for fewer chain segments converts into IPC even at higher Ir. Neither metric alone predicted this; the arbiter was cycles-at-equal-Instr plus interleaved wall. The scheduling is load-bearing: extracting the transition fields and building the sequence *before* the read (entries/bases die early) loses 5% cycles (falsified, [negative](../negative.md)) — the add-bits extraction must stay in the tail behind the states.

- **read-side fold family falsified (2026-09-20)**: both remaining levers of the fused bit-read's family — carrying the raw container (read count folds the consumed offset; reload shift-free) and folding `consumed` into a single bit cursor `u = N−57` (reload = plain load at `src + (u'>>3)`) — deliver their predicted uop cuts (json Ir −2.1% / flat) and win the throughput-bound shapes (json −1.8% cycles, dll −1.1%, skewed stream −2..−4%), but lose text reproducibly (+1.4..+3.5% cycles, +2.7..+5.0% stream wall across two independent allocation re-rolls): moving the consumed offset from the window into the count puts the loop-carried cursor in series with `n` on the read's serial chain, and the latency-leaning long-sequence mix pays that beyond what the removed shifts buy. The pre-shifted window's count-from-`n` is load-bearing; the per-shape bar rejects the trade (data in [negative](../negative.md)). Attribution from the same profiling round: on text.zst3/9 the untouched xxh64 absorb is 52-60% of `prof dec` cycles and the fused loop 11-21% (low-sample profiles) — text-stream wall barely moves for loop-side wins, while loop-side losses reproduce at full size.

## Executor (port of the libzstd wildcopy copy stack)

- literals: ≤16 inline copy16 (`literals_buffer.reserve(16)` guarantees the over-read stays in bounds), \>16 uses a 16B chunk loop.
- match: offset≥16 → copy16/16B loop; [8,16) 8B loop; <8 overlapCopy8 (dec32/dec64 tables write 8B at a time to lift the effective offset to ≥8; **first 4 bytes byte-by-byte** so stores feed loads).
- budget gate `w+ll+ml+16 <= out_len` guarantees overshoot lands in a legal region; the tail takes the exact slow path.
- motivation: json/skewed are masses of 5-6B small copies (LD_PRELOAD measured 32MiB with ~3M PLT calls, ~35% of time). After landing, memmove fell 14.3%→1.6%, json/skewed/text decoding +23-45%. `8ce527a`
- active-segment match source algebraically simplified to `dst - offset` (virtual distance = physical distance, base indirection eliminated); wrapped sources outlined #[cold] to shed 4 view live values; boundary checks merged 3-in-1. `bee924d`
- prev-segment linear fast path: a wrapped previous-segment source physically sits at `dst + seg_a_end - offset` (above the write cursor by ≥MAX_BLOCK, so wildcopy double-sided overshoot is safe); one wildcopy replaces generic segment walking; **placed in the existing out-of-line callee to preserve hot-loop codegen**. skewed.zst9 +4.8%. `6bccc54`

## Huffman literals

- X2 double-symbol table (port of libzstd HUF_decompress4X2) + custom **80% pairing threshold** (the pairable fraction of code space is computed from weights; below it the space is left empty and X1 is used): skewed.zst1 +43%, skewed.zst3 +15%. `0af7fc1`
- X1/X2 stream-state pointerization (ip/op fold base, live values 16→14): inner-loop instructions -10%. **Conclusion: at the practical floor** — libzstd's hand-written asm does 3 dtable loads (memport-bound ~0.7 cyc/B) vs our 1 load + 2 shifts (ALU-bound ~0.75 cyc/B), same magnitude. `5be5f88`

## Residual (vs libzstd streaming, json/dll 1.2-1.4×, skewed 1.1-1.3×, text 1.04-1.2×)

**Differentially attributed 2026-09-20** ([dec-gap](../bench/dec-gap.md)): against libzstd 1.5.7 on the same files we execute 1.34-1.41× the instructions at equal-or-better IPC with equal L1d misses — the loop is throughput-bound on both sides and the entire cycle gap is instruction volume (json 174 vs 126 Ir/seq, +48; the loop alone is 82% of the cycle gap, non-loop fixed costs are at parity). The +48 decomposes: entry unpacking +10 (3 packed loads + 24 extract shifts vs their 12 field loads), offset-history machinery +12 on real offsets, stack round-trips +15 (win store/load, consumed bookkeeping, double state stores, rem/op spills), read+splits +11, literals +2. text is the exception — fewer instructions than zstd, latency-bound in the copy tails (57 K seq/32 MiB at avg ml 311).

The register wall is proven (see negative); every structural rework including the ring pipeline is falsified. The 2026-09-20 rounds add the harshest form of the re-roll law: the read-side serial-chain folds (raw-container read fold, single-cursor consumed-into-ip) win the dense shapes ~2% yet reproducibly cost text 3-5% wall — folding the consumed offset into the count puts the loop cursor on the read's serial chain, and the pre-shifted window's count-from-n is load-bearing for the long-sequence mix; two surgical Ir cuts (real-offset constant rotation, −8.7 Ir/seq; 5-bit nb fields) both lost at the same wall (the first to a +3-5% text-stream placement tax, the second deterministically at Ir +1.6%). Per-sequence attrition below ~10 Ir/seq no longer has positive expected value standalone; the fused bit-read (+4.6% Ir for 2% wall) remains the only proven conversion. Remaining levers ranked in [dec-gap](../bench/dec-gap.md): the entry-struct layout swap is falsified too (2026-09-20, both the field-per-load and the 2×u32 field-group variants — loads-as-extracts cut 15 Ir/seq and bought no cycles because IPC fell 1:1 with the instruction cut on every shape; see negative), the offset-history fold has no ≥5% bundling partner left, and text's copy-tail latency is latency-class with no Ir lever. With the read-side folds and both entry layouts gone, the fused loop's residual vs libzstd has no open instruction-volume lever — the loop is issue/latency-saturated, so only structural (latency-class) candidates remain.
